How It Works

Experience a seamless flow from identifying a public issue to its complete resolution by the specific authorized municipal department.

01

Spot & Report

Identify an issue in your locality—be it infrastructure, water, or electricity. Snap a picture and securely submit a ticket in exactly 30 seconds.

02

Intelligent Routing

The platform instantly categorizes and routes your complaint to the precise municipal department logically responsible for resolving it.

03

Track & Resolve

Departments update the live status tracking module as they work. You are notified the moment your civic issue is permanently resolved.
